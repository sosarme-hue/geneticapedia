Geneticapedia **does not use tracking cookies or store personal data**. Quiz answers are processed only in your browser and are never sent to a server.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address under its own privacy policy. The hosting provider (Hostinger) may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operation.

If we ever add visitor statistics or advertising, they will only run with your consent and we will explain it here.
