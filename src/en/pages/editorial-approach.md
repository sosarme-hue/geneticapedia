## Sources in every article

Every article cites peer-reviewed papers, clinical guidelines and reference bodies such as the National Human Genome Research Institute, MedlinePlus Genetics, Orphanet, the World Health Organization and the European Medicines Agency.

## Proven, researched, sold

We clearly separate what is already used in clinical practice, what is still being researched and what is sold commercially without enough evidence.

## No determinism, no alarmism

Genes matter, but they rarely decide everything. We present probabilities as probabilities, not destinies, and give figures in context.
