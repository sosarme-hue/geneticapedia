*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

Geneticapedia **does not set its own or third-party cookies** while you browse, and uses no analytics or advertising. That is why we do not show a cookie banner.

You can view, delete or block cookies in your browser settings, usually under privacy.

If we ever add non-essential cookies, such as statistics or advertising, we will ask for your permission first and update this page.
