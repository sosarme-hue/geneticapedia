## Who this guide is for

Geneticapedia is written for the general public, with scientific sources cited in every article. That makes it useful as **supporting material** in class. Here are three routes that follow the genetics content of secondary school Biology. Each can be covered in 3-6 lessons.

All articles are free, need no registration and have a Spanish version, which is useful in bilingual schools.

## Route 1: age 15-16. Genetic information and inheritance

**Aim**: understand what DNA is, how traits are inherited and solve simple inheritance problems.

| Lesson | Read | To discuss |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [What is DNA](../../basics/what-is-dna/) | Why are four letters enough to store so much information? |
| 2 | [Genes, chromosomes and genome](../../basics/genes-chromosomes-and-genome/) | Count the chromosomes in a karyotype |
| 3 | [Mendel's laws](../../heredity/mendels-laws/) | Pea cross problems |
| 4 | [Blood groups](../../everyday-traits/blood-groups/) | Can two group A parents have a group O child? |
| 5 | [Inheritance patterns](../../heredity/inheritance-patterns/) | Interpret a pedigree |
| 6 | [Eye colour](../../everyday-traits/eye-colour/) | Why is the single-gene model not enough? |

Extras: the [quiz](../../quiz/) and the [history of genetics](../../history/).

## Route 3: health vocational courses and nursing

**Aim**: know the most common genetic conditions and how they are diagnosed, to communicate better with patients and families.

1. [Types of genetic tests](../../diagnosis/types-of-genetic-tests/) and [How to read a genetic result](../../diagnosis/how-to-read-a-genetic-result/).
2. [Newborn and prenatal screening](../../diagnosis/newborn-and-prenatal-screening/): the heel-prick test.
3. Three condition fact sheets to choose from: [cystic fibrosis](../../diseases/cystic-fibrosis/), [Down syndrome](../../diseases/down-syndrome/), [familial hypercholesterolaemia](../../diseases/familial-hypercholesterolaemia/).
4. [Genetic counselling](../../diagnosis/genetic-counselling/) and [Support and disability recognition](../../rare-diseases/support-and-disability-recognition/).
5. [Pharmacogenetics](../../treatments/pharmacogenetics/).

## Classroom tips

- Ask each group to look up **one source** from the list at the end of an article and explain what kind of document it is: a clinical trial, a review, a guideline.
- Use the [glossary](../../glossary/) as a vocabulary reference.
- Remind students that the medical information on the site **does not replace** professional advice, especially if a topic affects them personally.
