## Why debate genetics

Genetics raises questions with no single right answer: they depend on values, laws and how risks and benefits are shared. Debating them helps students **tell facts from opinions**, use reliable sources and listen to other views. They suit upper secondary Biology, Philosophy or Science and Society classes.

## Ground rules

1. Every argument rests on **a fact or a source**.
2. Criticise ideas, never people.
3. Avoid personal or family health stories unless someone chooses to share them.
4. At the end, each group summarises the **opposing** view as fairly as possible.

## Eight questions

### 1. Should editing human embryo DNA be allowed?

Read: [Ethics of gene editing](../../treatments/ethics-of-gene-editing/) and [CRISPR explained](../../treatments/crispr-explained/). Prompts: the 2018 edited babies in China, treatment versus enhancement, consent of future generations.

### 2. Is an ancestry test worth it?

Read: [Is an ancestry test worth it?](../../faq/is-an-ancestry-test-worth-it/) and [Genetic data privacy](../../genetics-and-society/genetic-data-privacy/).

### 3. Should police be able to search consumer genetic databases for suspects?

Read: [Genetic genealogy](../../forensic-genetics/genetic-genealogy/) and [Police DNA databases](../../genetics-and-society/police-dna-databases/).

### 4. Should Europe grow more gene-edited crops?

Read: [GM and gene-edited crops](../../plants-and-animals/gm-and-gene-edited-crops/) and [Golden rice](../../plants-and-animals/golden-rice/).

### 5. Is it acceptable to release gene-drive mosquitoes to end malaria?

Read: [Modified mosquitoes against malaria](../../plants-and-animals/modified-mosquitoes-against-malaria/).

### 6. Which genetic tests should every newborn have?

Read: [Newborn and prenatal screening](../../diagnosis/newborn-and-prenatal-screening/) and [Spinal muscular atrophy](../../diseases/spinal-muscular-atrophy/). Prompts: benefits of early detection, conditions with no treatment, cost and parental anxiety.

### 7. Can genes be patented?

Read: [Can genes be patented?](../../genetics-and-society/can-genes-be-patented/) and [Feng Zhang](../../people/feng-zhang/).

### 8. Do human races exist?

Read: [Why biological races do not exist](../../human-evolution/why-biological-races-do-not-exist/) and [Out of Africa](../../human-evolution/out-of-africa/). Prompts: the difference between genetic variation and social categories.

## Assessment

A simple rubric can score: use of sources (40 %), clarity of arguments (30 %), listening and responding to the other side (20 %) and following the rules (10 %).
