title: Can I change my genes with diet or exercise?
desc: Your DNA sequence does not change, but how your genes are used does. What epigenetics says and what it does not.
---
## Short answer

**Your DNA sequence, no.** The letters you inherited stay the same throughout life (apart from errors some cells accumulate). But you **can influence how many genes are used** and, above all, how much your genes weigh on your health.

## A little more

- **Epigenetics** studies chemical marks that switch genes on or off without changing their sequence. Smoking, diet, exercise and stress change some of these marks. See [epigenetics](../../heredity/epigenetics/).
- In most common diseases (diabetes, heart attack, many cancers), genes **raise or lower risk**, but habits count a great deal. A study of more than 55,000 people showed that, among those at high genetic risk of heart attack, a healthy lifestyle cut their risk almost in half.
- In serious **single-gene** conditions such as [cystic fibrosis](../../diseases/cystic-fibrosis/), lifestyle does not prevent the disease, although good care greatly improves its course. The classic exception is [phenylketonuria](../../diseases/phenylketonuria/), where a diet completely prevents the damage.

## Beware of promises

Be wary of anyone selling diets "based on your DNA" or supplements to "reprogramme your genes". The evidence that a diet based on genetic tests works better than general healthy eating is **very weak**.
