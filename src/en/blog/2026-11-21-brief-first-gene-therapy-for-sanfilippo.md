**What happened.** On **17 September 2026** the FDA approved **Fayuvi** (rebisufligene etisparvovec), from Ultragenyx. It is the first authorised treatment for **Sanfilippo syndrome type A** (mucopolysaccharidosis IIIA), a [rare disease](../../rare-diseases/what-is-a-rare-disease/) with [recessive inheritance](../../heredity/inheritance-patterns/) in which an enzyme is missing and sugars build up in the brain.

**What it is.** A single-dose [gene therapy](../../treatments/gene-therapy/) that delivers a healthy copy of the gene. It is meant for children whose neurological development is still preserved, so early diagnosis is key.

**The number.** In the Transpher A trial, treated children scored on average **23.5 points higher** on cognitive tests than 27 untreated children from a natural history study.

**Good to know.** The label warns of liver and blood risks and requires steroids before and for at least eight weeks after. Affected families helped fund the research for years. It is not approved in Europe.
