**What happened.** On **18 November 2026** the FDA approved **Livsayis** (cemdisiran), from Regeneron, for adults with **generalized myasthenia gravis** who have antibodies against the acetylcholine receptor. It is an autoimmune disease, not an inherited one: the immune system attacks the junction between nerve and muscle, causing weakness and fatigue.

**What it is.** A **small interfering RNA** (siRNA). It does not change DNA. It delivers a fragment of RNA to the liver that gets the message of the **complement C5** gene destroyed. C5 is a protein involved in the damage to muscle. It is the step [from gene to protein](../../basics/from-gene-to-protein/), interrupted on purpose. It is injected under the skin **every three months**.

**Why it matters.** It is the first siRNA approved for myasthenia gravis and has the longest dosing interval available. It shows that tools born from genetics also work for diseases that are not genetic.

**Worth knowing.** In the NIMBLE trial, published in *The Lancet*, the improvement on the daily-activities scale was 2.3 points greater than with placebo. It carries a warning about **serious meningococcal infections** and requires vaccination first. It is not approved in Europe.
