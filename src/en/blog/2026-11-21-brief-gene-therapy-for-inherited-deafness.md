title: In brief: the first gene therapy for inherited deafness
desc: Otarmeni, approved in the US in April 2026, restores hearing to people with variants in the OTOF gene.
---
**What happened.** On **23 April 2026** the FDA granted accelerated approval to **Otarmeni** (lunsotogene parvec), from Regeneron. It is the first [gene therapy](../../treatments/gene-therapy/) for an inherited form of deafness.

**Who it is for.** People born profoundly deaf because of variants in both copies of the ***OTOF*** gene, which makes otoferlin. Without it, the cells of the inner ear cannot pass the signal on to the auditory nerve.

**How it works.** The gene is too big for a single virus, so it is split between **two AAV vectors**, infused once into the cochlea, which rejoin inside the cell.

**The number.** In the CHORD trial, 16 of 20 participants (**80 %**) had improved hearing.

**Good to know.** The approval depends on confirming the benefit with more data, and it only helps one specific kind of deafness, so a [genetic test](../../diagnosis/types-of-genetic-tests/) is needed. Regeneron provides it free to eligible US patients. It is not approved in Europe.
