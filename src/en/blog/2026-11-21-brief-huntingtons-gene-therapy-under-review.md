**What happened.** In early November 2026 the US FDA accepted uniQure's marketing application for **AMT-130** (ifezuntirgene inilparvovec) for [Huntington's disease](../../diseases/huntingtons-disease/) and granted it **priority review**. The target decision date is **1 May 2027**. The company has also filed in the United Kingdom.

**What it is.** A [gene therapy](../../treatments/gene-therapy/) injected once into the brain, by surgery, which aims to lower the huntingtin protein that damages neurons.

**Why it matters.** If approved, it would be the first treatment aimed at the cause of the disease. The application rests on a small trial followed for three years and compared with patients from a registry, not with a placebo group. That is why the FDA had doubts in 2025, and it will probably ask an expert committee for advice.

**Good to know.** It is not available outside trials and is not authorised in Europe.
