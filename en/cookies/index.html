Cookie policy

Geneticapedia only uses statistics cookies (Google Analytics) if you accept them.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Geneticapedia does not use advertising cookies. It only uses Google Analytics statistics cookies, and only if you accept them in the notice shown on your first visit. If you reject them or make no choice, Google Analytics is not loaded.

Cookies we use

  • Your cookie choice (necessary). Stored in your browser (local storage, key aviso-cookies) for 12 months so we do not ask again. It is never sent to a server.
  • Google Analytics (statistics, only with your permission). Cookies _ga and _ga_0Q79VT1T6F, set by Google Ireland Ltd., lasting up to 2 years. They tell us, in aggregate, which pages are visited and where visits come from. Google may process this data outside the EU with the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time in “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, Google Analytics stops being used and its cookies are deleted.

You can also view, delete or block cookies in your browser settings, usually under privacy.

If we add other non-essential cookies, we will ask for your permission first and update this page.

Geneticapedia

An encyclopedia of genetics for the general public: science, health and society. It does not provide diagnosis or individual genetic counselling.

Worried about a condition that runs in your family? Talk to your family doctor, who can refer you to a clinical genetics or genetic counselling service.
