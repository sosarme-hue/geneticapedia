About

Geneticapedia grew out of primary care and the questions genetics raises for patients.

Alejandro Rosàs Navarro

I am a family physician and a specialist in Preventive Medicine and Public Health, with over 38 years in primary care, registered with the Barcelona Medical Association under number 28574.

In my practice I have watched genetics move from the laboratory into everyday life: newborn screening, prenatal screening, inherited cancer, ancestry kits and headlines about CRISPR. Patients' questions deserve clear answers, with sources and without alarmism.

Geneticapedia aims to make genetics accessible to everyone: what we know, how we know it and what we still don't know.

What it is and what it is not

Geneticapedia is a general-audience encyclopedia. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, interpretation of individual results or genetic counselling, and it does not replace care from health professionals.
