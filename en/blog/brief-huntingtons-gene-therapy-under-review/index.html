In brief: FDA to fast-track review of the first gene therapy for Huntington's disease

The US agency accepted the application for AMT-130 and will decide by 1 May 2027. It is not approved yet.

What happened. In early November 2026 the US FDA accepted uniQure's marketing application for AMT-130 (ifezuntirgene inilparvovec) for Huntington's disease and granted it priority review. The target decision date is 1 May 2027. The company has also filed in the United Kingdom.

What it is. A gene therapy injected once into the brain, by surgery, which aims to lower the huntingtin protein that damages neurons.

Why it matters. If approved, it would be the first treatment aimed at the cause of the disease. The application rests on a small trial followed for three years and compared with patients from a registry, not with a placebo group. That is why the FDA had doubts in 2025, and it will probably ask an expert committee for advice.

Good to know. It is not available outside trials and is not authorised in Europe.
